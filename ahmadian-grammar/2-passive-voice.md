### 1. Simple Present

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + am/is/are + past participle (V₃) + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room is cleaned every day.**  

---

### 2. Present Continuous

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + am/is/are + being + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room is being cleaned right now.**  

---

### 3. Simple Past

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + was/were + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room was cleaned yesterday.**  

---

### 4. Past Continuous

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + was/were + being + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room was being cleaned when I arrived.**  

---

### 5. Present Perfect

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + have/has + been + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room has been cleaned already.**  

---

### 6. Past Perfect

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + had + been + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room had been cleaned before the guests came.**  

---

### 7. Future Simple

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + will + be + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room will be cleaned tomorrow.**  

---

### 8. Future Perfect

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + will + have + been + past participle + ...`

**✅ Example**  
- **The room will have been cleaned by 6 PM.**  

---

### 9. Modal Verbs

**🏗️ Structure**  

`Subject + modal + be + past participle + ...`

**✅ Examples**  
- **The room can be cleaned easily.**  
- **The work must be finished today.**  
- **The letter should be sent now.**  

---

## ⚠️ Important Note

The following tenses are **practically never used in the passive voice** in English (or are extremely rare and unnatural):

- **Future Continuous**: e.g. `will be being cleaned` can be formed, but nobody uses it.
- **Present Perfect Continuous**: e.g. `has been being cleaned` is grammatically correct but is never used.
- **Past Perfect Continuous**: e.g. `had been being cleaned` is completely unnatural.
- **Future Perfect Continuous**: e.g. `will have been being cleaned` only exists in grammar books, not in real conversation or writing.

✅ **Solution:** Instead of these tenses, use the **active voice** or **simpler passive tenses**.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

- **Object of active** → becomes **subject of passive**  
  *(Active: I clean the room. → Passive: The room is cleaned.)*

- **By + agent** → only if needed  
  *Example: The cake was made **by my mom**.*

- **No passive with:** *happen, sleep, seem, become* (intransitive verbs)  
  *(You cannot say "The accident is happened" — it's incorrect.)*
