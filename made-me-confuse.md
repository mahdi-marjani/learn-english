# 🧠 Made Me Confuse

A running list of words, sounds, and grammar points that tripped me up while learning English — from day one until now.

---

## 🔊 Homophones & Near-Homophones
- **least** vs **list**
- **hole** vs **whole**
- **boss** vs **bus**
- **chip** vs **cheap**
- **sleep** vs **slip**
- **too** vs **two** vs **to**
- **fair** vs **fare**
- **suppose** vs **spouse**
- **soup** vs **soap** vs **swap**
- **module** vs **nodule** — just one letter apart, but their phonetics are completely different
- **until** vs **Intel** — sound alike, mean nothing alike

## 🗣️ Tricky Pronunciation
- **adequate** — /ˈæd.ə.kwət/
- **queue** — /kjuː/
- **cease** — /siːs/
- **chaos** — /ˈkeɪ.ɑːs/
- The **"-ough"** family, all pronounced differently: *though, thought, through, thorough, throughout, throw, taught, although*

---

## 👀 Same Spelling, Different Sound
- **read** (base form) vs **read** (past form)
- **increase** (verb) vs **increase** (noun) — the stress moves

## 📍 Prepositions
- **in vs at** — *I live **in** London.* / *I am **at** the station.*
- **on** — *work **on** the phone* ✅, not "work with the phone" ❌
- **for** (duration) — *I've been writing this note **for** 2 hours now.*
- **to** — *He talked **to** Tom* ✅, not "He talked with Tom" ❌
- **on** = about/regarding — *a chapter **on** deployment* = a chapter *about* deployment
- **otherwise** = else, etc.
- Easy to mix up in general: **in, to, by, of, on, at, as**

*(Full breakdown: `vs/in-vs-at.md`)*

## 📐 Grammar Points
- **this/that** (singular) vs **these/those** (plural)
- *He feels good* — in Persian: *he has a good feeling*
- **nothing** vs **no**: *I do nothing.* / *I have no money.* — in Persian: *I don't do anything* / *I don't have any money*
- *She's got a headache* — **'s** here means **has**, not "is"
- **everything** vs **anything**
