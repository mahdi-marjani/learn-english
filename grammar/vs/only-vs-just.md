# 📘 Only vs Just (Cheat Sheet)

They overlap in "no more than / merely," but **just** has extra meanings, and **only** feels more exclusive.

---

## 1. ONLY
**Meaning:** No more/no other than the thing mentioned.
**Examples:**
- I have **only** two dollars.
- **Only** she can fix this.

## 2. JUST
**Meaning:** Merely/simply, or "a moment ago," or "exactly."
**Examples:**
- It's **just** a scratch. *(= only)*
- I **just** got home. *(= a moment ago)*

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | ONLY | JUST |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "recently" meaning | ❌ | ✅ |
| "exactly" meaning | ❌ | ✅ |
| Position changes meaning | Strongly | Less so |
