# 📘 That vs What (Cheat Sheet)

**What** already means "the thing(s) which," so it never needs a noun before it. **That** does.

---

## 1. THAT
**Meaning:** Refers back to a noun, or introduces a statement (as a conjunction).
**Examples:**
- This is the book **that** I told you about.
- I believe **that** you can do it.

## 2. WHAT
**Meaning:** Means "the thing(s) which" — used with no noun before it. Also the question word.
**Examples:**
- **What** you said really helped me.
- **What** is your name?

⚠️ ❌ *This is that I need.* → ✅ This is **what** I need.

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | THAT | WHAT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Needs a noun before it | Yes | No |
| Used in questions | No | Yes |
