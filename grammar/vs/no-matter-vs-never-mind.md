# 📘 No Matter vs Never Mind (Cheat Sheet)

Both can sit right before a question word (*what, who, how...*), which is exactly why they get mixed up — but **no matter** means "regardless of," while **never mind** means "don't worry about it / ignore it."

---

## 1. NO MATTER
**Meaning:** "Regardless of" — the result stays the same, whatever the condition.
**Examples:**
- **No matter** what happens, I'll support you.
- **No matter** how hard I try, I can't do it.

## 2. NEVER MIND
**Meaning:** "Don't worry about it" / "forget it" — dismisses something as unimportant.
**Examples:**
- **Never mind**, it doesn't matter.
- **Never mind** what he said — just relax.

⚠️ Same pattern, different meaning: *No matter what he said, I still trust him* (= regardless, an outcome that won't change) vs *Never mind what he said* (= ignore it, ​it isn't worth worrying about).

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | NO MATTER | NEVER MIND |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Meaning | Regardless of | Don't worry / ignore it |
| Followed by | what/who/how/where + clause, or *if* | noun, -ing, a clause, or used alone |
| Can stand alone? | No — needs a clause | Yes — *"Never mind."* |
