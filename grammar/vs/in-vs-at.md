# 📘 In vs At (Cheat Sheet)

**In** = inside a larger area or longer time. **At** = an exact point.

---

## 1. IN
**Meaning:** Larger area (countries, cities, rooms) or longer time (months, years, seasons).
**Examples:**
- She lives **in** Paris.
- I was born **in** 2001.

## 2. AT
**Meaning:** A specific point (address, event) or exact time (clock times, night).
**Examples:**
- Meet me **at** the entrance.
- The train leaves **at** 9 o'clock.

⚠️ **at night** is the exception — a point, not a period, unlike *in the morning/evening*.

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | IN | AT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Place | Larger/enclosed area | Specific point |
| Time | Longer period | Exact point |
| Example | in June, in London | at 5 pm, at night |
