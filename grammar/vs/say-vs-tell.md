- Andrew **said** he was sick.

**In general:**

| Verb | Pattern |
| :--- | :--- |
| **say** | say + **something** |
| **tell** | tell + **someone** |

---

## 1. SAY

**Meaning:** to express something in words.

`say` is an **irregular verb**.

| Infinitive | Present tense | Past tense | Past participle | Present participle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| to say | say / says | said | said | saying |

- ✅ He **said** hello.
- ❌ He **said** me hello.
- ✅ He **said** hello **to** me.

**Examples:**

- How do you **say** chicken in Spanish?
- I **said** goodbye to him.
- We didn't **say** anything.
- Cecilia was **saying** the same thing.

---

## 2. TELL

**Pattern:** you tell someone something → `tell + someone (+ something)`

`tell` is an **irregular verb**.

| Infinitive | Present tense | Past tense | Past participle | Present participle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| to tell | tell / tells | told | told | telling |

- ✅ He **said** his name.
- ❌ He **said** me his name.
- ✅ He **told** me his name.

**Examples:**

- She **told** me her name.
- He **told** us his secret.
- Can you **tell** me the time please?
- The teacher **told** them to put their books away.

### Commands or instructions

`tell + someone + to + verb`

- I **told** Steve **to be** quiet.
- They **told** my sister **to turn** her music down.

### Tell with "WH" words

`tell + someone + WH-word (where / when / how / what...) + ...`

- They **told** us **where** to find the hotel.
- He **told** me **how** to fix my computer.
- Can you **tell** me **when** you are ready?
- I **told** you **what** happened.

---

## 3. Always TELL: truth, lie, joke, story

With these four words, use **tell** — never *say*. The person (*me / us / you*) is optional.

### tell + the truth

- ✅ I want you to **tell** me the truth.
- ✅ I want you to **tell** the truth.
- ❌ I want you to **say** the truth.

### tell + a lie (lies)

- ✅ Amanda has never **told** me a lie.
- ✅ Amanda has never **told** a lie.
- ❌ Amanda has never **said** a lie.

### tell + a joke

- ✅ John **told** us a terrible joke.
- ✅ John **told** a terrible joke.
- ❌ John **said** a terrible joke.

### tell + a story

- ✅ I'm going to **tell** you a story about trolls.
- ✅ I'm going to **tell** a story about trolls.
- ❌ I'm going to **say** a story about trolls.

---

## 🔍 Quick Summary

| Feature | SAY | TELL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pattern | say + **something** | tell + **someone** (+ something) |
| Person right after the verb? | No (needs **to**): *said hello **to** me* | Yes: *told **me** his name* |
| Forms | say / says – **said** – **said** – saying | tell / tells – **told** – **told** – telling |
| truth / lie / joke / story | ❌ | ✅ |
