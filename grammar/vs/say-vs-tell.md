# 📘 Say vs Tell (Cheat Sheet)

The difference is whether a **person** comes right after the verb.

---

## 1. SAY
**Meaning:** To speak words — focus on **the words**.
**Examples:**
- She **said** hello.
- I **said** to her, "Wait for me."

⚠️ ❌ *She said me hello.* → ✅ She said hello **to** me.

## 2. TELL
**Meaning:** To inform someone — focus on **the listener**.
**Examples:**
- She **told** me the truth.
- I **told** him to wait.

---

## 📌 Fixed Expressions

| SAY | TELL |
| :--- | :--- |
| say hello / goodbye | tell a story |
| say sorry | tell a lie / the truth |

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | SAY | TELL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Personal object right after | No (needs "to") | Yes |
| Pattern | say something **to** someone | tell someone something |
