# 📘 Also vs Too (Cheat Sheet)

Both mean "in addition" — the real difference is **position**.

---

## 1. ALSO
**Meaning:** Adds a fact; used mid-sentence, before the main verb (or after be/aux).
**Examples:**
- I **also** speak French.
- She is **also** a doctor.

## 2. TOO
**Meaning:** Same idea, used at the **end** of a sentence. (*As well* works the same way.)
**Examples:**
- I speak French **too**.
- Can I come **too**?

⚠️ Negative sentences use **either**, not too/also: *I don't like it either.*

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | ALSO | TOO |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Position | Mid-sentence | End of sentence |
| Negative form | either | either |
