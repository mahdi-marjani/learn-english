# 📘 Within vs Inside (Cheat Sheet)

**Inside** is physical and concrete; **within** is about limits (time, distance, amount) or formal/abstract space.

---

## 1. INSIDE
**Meaning:** Physically in the interior of something — opposite of *outside*.
**Examples:**
- The keys are **inside** the drawer.
- Come **inside**, it's cold outside.

## 2. WITHIN
**Meaning:** Not exceeding a limit (time/distance/amount), or inside a system/group/feeling.
**Examples:**
- Please reply **within** 3 days.
- There are disagreements **within** the team.

---

## 🔍 Comparison

| Feature | INSIDE | WITHIN |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Type | Physical, concrete | Limits or abstract space |
| Common use | inside the box | within a week, within the company |
