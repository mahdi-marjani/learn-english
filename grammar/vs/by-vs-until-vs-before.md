# 📘 By vs Until vs Before

## 1. By
When we are talking about a time when we must finish something, or the latest time that something can happen, we use `by`

**Examples:**  
I have to finish this homework by Friday

## 2. Until
When we are talking about something that continuous up to a certain time or event, we use `until`

**Examples:**  
The library is open until 5pm  
Keep working until you have finished all the exercises

## 3. Before
`Before` means earlier (than)

**Examples:**  
I won't be ready before 5pm (= I might be ready at 5.00, 5.05, 5.30, etc. but certainly not earlier than 5.00)

**Compare:**  
I won't be ready until 5pm (= I will certainly be ready at 5pm, but not earlier)
