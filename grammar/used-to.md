---

'Used to' shows that something happened **regularly** or **often** in the past, or was true for a period in the past, but **not any more**.

---

## 1. Past Habitual Actions

'Used to' talks about something you did **regularly** in the past, but **don't do anymore**. Examples:

- I `used to` play the piano when I was a child.
- They `used to` go camping every summer.

---

## 2. Past States

'Used to' also talks about something that **was true** in the past, but **isn't true anymore**. Examples:

- There `used to` be a bookstore here.
- She `used to` be very shy.

---

## 3. Negation and Questions

To make **negatives** and **questions**, use the auxiliary **did** (see [auxiliary verbs](auxiliary-verbs.md)).

**Did** already shows the past, so 'used to' loses the **-d** and becomes **'use to'**.

Examples:

- `Did` he <ins>use to</ins> smoke?
- `Didn't` she <ins>use to</ins> go to our school?
- I `didn't` <ins>use to</ins> like olives.
- She `didn't` <ins>use to</ins> live here.

**Exception:** if the sentence is negative because of a word like **never** or **nobody**, don't use **did**. Keep **used to**:

- We `never` <ins>used to</ins> worry about money.

---

## 4. Be/Get Used to

After `be` or `get`, **used to** has a **different meaning**: you are **familiar with** something, so it feels **normal** to you. It is **not** about past habits, and you can use it in the **past, present, or future**. Compare:

- I `used to` get up early every day.
  - This shows a **habit in the past**.
- I `am` <ins>used to</ins> getting up early.
  - Getting up early feels **normal** to me. Notice that a **gerund** (-ing) follows 'used to' here.
- You'll eventually `get` <ins>used to</ins> getting up early.
  - It will start to feel **normal**. This is **not** a past habit.

---

## Review

`Used to` has two meanings, both in the past:

1. an activity that you did regularly in the past
2. a state that was true in the past, but not any longer

In both cases, it needs the auxiliary 'did' to form questions and negative sentences:

- She `used to` have long hair.
- She `didn't use to` have long hair.
- `Did` she `use to` have long hair?
