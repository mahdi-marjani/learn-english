An **auxiliary verb** (helping verb) goes with a main verb to help form tenses, questions, negatives, and more.

---

## 1. Primary Auxiliary

The three primary auxiliary verbs: **be**, **do**, **have**.

### `be`

**Forms:** be, am, is, are, was, were, being, been

**Forms the continuous tense:**

- She **is** **working** from home.
- It **was** **raining** heavily last night.

**Forms the passive voice:**

- Her bike **was** **stolen**.
- The bird **was** **taken** to the vet.

### `do`

**Forms:** do, does, did, doing, done

**Expresses emphasis:**

- I **do** **want** dinner.
- She **does** **like** dancing.

**Forms questions:**

- **Does** Nathalie **study** Italian?
- **Do** you **know** the time?

**Negative statements:**

- Nathalie **does not** **study** Italian.
- I **do not** **know** the time.

### `have`

**Forms:** have, has, had, having

**Forms perfect tenses:**

- We **have** been to India. *(present perfect)*
- He **had** left home already. *(past perfect)*
- I will **have** arrived by then. *(future perfect)*

## 2. Modal Auxiliary

**Modal verbs don't change their form.**

### `Likelihood`

**Modals:** can, could, may, might, would

- It **might** rain over the weekend.

### `Ability`

**Modals:** can, could

- She **can** run faster than me.

### `Permission`

**Modals:** can, could, may

- You **may** swim in the pool.

### `Intention`

**Modals:** shall, will

- I **shall** leave the party early.

### `Request`

**Modals:** can, could, may, might, will, would

- **Would** you look after my dog?

### `Necessity`

**Modals:** should, must, ought to

- He **must** remember their anniversary.
