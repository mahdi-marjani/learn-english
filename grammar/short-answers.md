---

Answering a question by saying just **Yes** or **No** can be perceived as too direct and impolite. If you wish to sound more polite, add a **short answer**

---

## 1. Present Tense

- `Do` you play tennis?
- Yes, I `do`.
- No, I `don't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Don't` you play tennis?
- Yes, I `do`.
- No, I `don't`.

---

## 2. Past Tense

- `Did` you finish your homework?
- Yes, I `did`.
- No, I `didn't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Didn't` you finish your homework?
- Yes, I `did`.
- No, I `didn't`.

---

## 3. Present Perfect

- `Have` you seen that movie?
- Yes, I `have`.
- No, I `haven't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Haven't` you seen that movie?
- Yes, I `have`.
- No, I `haven't`.

## 4. Modals

### can

- `Can` you swim?
- Yes, I `can`.
- No, I `can't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Can't` you swim?
- Yes, I `can`.
- No, I `can't`.

### will

- `Will` you come to the party?
- Yes, I `will`.
- No, I `won't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Won't` you come to the party?
- Yes, I `will`.
- No, I `won't`.

### should

- `Should` I call him?
- Yes, you `should`.
- No, you `shouldn't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Shouldn't` I call him?
- Yes, you `should`.
- No, you `shouldn't`.

## 5. Verb Be

### am

- `Are` you tired?
- Yes, I `am`.
- No, I `am not`.

**Negative question:**

- `Aren't` you tired?
- Yes, I `am`.
- No, I `am not`.

💡 Short form: **No, I'm not.** Never say *amn't*.

### is

- `Is` she at home?
- Yes, she `is`.
- No, she `isn't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Isn't` she at home?
- Yes, she `is`.
- No, she `isn't`.

### are

- `Are` they ready?
- Yes, they `are`.
- No, they `aren't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Aren't` they ready?
- Yes, they `are`.
- No, they `aren't`.

### was

- `Were` you at home last night?
- Yes, I `was`.
- No, I `wasn't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Weren't` you at home last night?
- Yes, I `was`.
- No, I `wasn't`.

### were

- `Were` they at the party?
- Yes, they `were`.
- No, they `weren't`.

**Negative question:**

- `Weren't` they at the party?
- Yes, they `were`.
- No, they `weren't`.
