A sentence can end with a preposition in two situations:

- Which gym do you go `to`? *(WH- question)*
- Alan is the boy I was telling you `about`! *(relative clause)*

---

## 1. WH- Questions

(What, Which, Who, Where, When...)

As we can see, the question and the answer are connected:

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Which** gym do you go `to`? | I **go** `to` the Superenergy gym. |
| Who did you **travel** `with`? | I **traveled** `with` my boyfriend. |
| What are you **worried** `about`? | I'm **worried** `about` my future. |

### Without a preposition at the end

| Preposition at the end | Preposition first |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Which** gym do you go `to`? | `To` **which** gym do you go? |
| **Who** did you travel `with`? | `With` **whom** did you travel? |
| **What** are you worried `about`? | `About` **what** are you worried? |

⚠️ The "preposition first" structure is too formal and rather outdated.

### Exam: WH- questions

Guess the WH- question for each answer:

| Answer | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| They're staying in my room. | Which room are they staying in? |
| I was laughing at his haircut. | What were you laughing at? |
| We should send it to the manager. | Who should we send it to? |

---

## 2. Relative Clauses

**Alan** is the boy I was telling you `about`!

The complete sentence: **Alan** is the boy (that/who) I was telling you `about`!

💡 These relative pronouns are sometimes omitted because they are not strictly necessary.

### From a simple sentence to a relative clause

| Simple sentence | Relative clause |
| :--- | :--- |
| I was **telling you** `about` **Alan**. | **Alan** is the boy (that/who) I was telling you `about`! |
| I'm **afraid** `of` **cockroaches**. | **Cockroaches** are the insects (that) **I'm afraid** `of`. |
| The iPhone **belongs** `to` **Apple**. | **Apple** is the company (that) the iPhone **belongs** `to`. |

### Exam: Relative clauses

Guess the relative clause with the preposition at the end:

| Sentence | Relative clause |
| :--- | :--- |
| I was angry at my boss. | My boss is the person (that) I was angry at. |
| I'm interested in psychology. | Psychology is the topic (that) I'm interested in. |
| You should pay attention to the road. | The road is the thing (that) you should pay attention to. |
