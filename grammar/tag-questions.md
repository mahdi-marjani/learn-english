A **tag question** is a short question added to the end of a sentence. It has two parts: the **sentence stem** and the **tag**.

---

## 1. Form

**Key:** $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{subject}}}$ · $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{verb}}}$ · $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{auxiliary verb}}}$

### Basic form

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + verb | verb + subject |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{She}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ tired, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{isn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{she}}}$?

### Pronoun or noun subject

The subject in the **tag** is always a **pronoun**.

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject (pronoun) + verb | verb + subject (pronoun) |
| subject (noun) + verb | verb + subject (pronoun) |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{They}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{are}}}$ hungry, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{aren't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?
- The $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{children}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{are}}}$ hungry, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{aren't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?

### Positive and negative

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + verb **(+)** | verb **(−)** + subject |
| subject + verb **(−)** | verb **(+)** + subject |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{He}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{speaks}}}$ French fluently, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{doesn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{he}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{He}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{doesn't speak}}}$ French fluently, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{does}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{he}}}$?

### Verb *be*

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + verb (be) | verb (be) + subject |

- This $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{pasta}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ delicious, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{isn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{it}}}$?
- The $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{baseball game}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{wasn't canceled}}}$, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{was}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{it}}}$?

### Simple present

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + verb (simple present) | verb (don't / do / doesn't / does) + subject |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{You}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{like}}}$ ice cream, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{don't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{They}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{don't like}}}$ broccoli, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{do}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{He}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{likes}}}$ pasta, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{doesn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{he}}}$?

### Simple past

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + verb (simple past) | verb (didn't / did) + subject |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{We}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{ran}}}$ for hours in gym class, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{didn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{we}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{You}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{didn't finish}}}$ your homework, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{did}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?

### Auxiliary verbs

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| subject + auxiliary verb + verb | auxiliary verb + subject |

- Your $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{neighbors}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{have}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{sold}}}$ their house, $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{haven't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{She}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{will}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{call}}}$ me back, $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{won't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{she}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{You}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{can't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{play}}}$ the guitar, $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{can}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?

## 2. Special Cases

### I am

The tag for **I am** is **aren't I?** (not *amn't I?*).

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| I am ... | aren't I? |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{I}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{am}}}$ late, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{aren't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{I}}}$?
- ❌ *I am late, amn't I?*

### Commands and suggestions

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| command (imperative) | will you? |
| Let's ... | shall we? |

- $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{Close}}}$ the door, $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{will}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{Let's go}}}$ to the beach, $\boxed{\color{#d09000}{\text{shall}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{we}}}$?

### Negative-meaning words

**never, nobody, nothing, hardly, rarely** count as **negative**, so the tag is **positive**.

| Sentence stem | Tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| negative-meaning word | verb (+) + subject |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{He}}}$ **never** $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{calls}}}$, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{does}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{he}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{You}}}$ **hardly** $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{know}}}$ him, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{do}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{She}}}$ **rarely** $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{eats}}}$ meat, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{does}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{she}}}$?

### Special subjects

| Subject in the stem | Subject in the tag |
| :--- | :--- |
| everyone, someone, nobody | they |
| nothing, something, this, that | it |
| there is | there (isn't there?) |

- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{Everyone}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{likes}}}$ pizza, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{don't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{Nobody}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{called}}}$, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{did}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{they}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{Something}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{smells}}}$ good, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{doesn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{it}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{Nothing}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ wrong, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{it}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{That}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ your car, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{isn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{it}}}$?
- $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{There}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{is}}}$ a problem, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{isn't}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{there}}}$?

### Same-polarity tags

Usually the tag is the **opposite** of the stem. A **positive** stem with a **positive** tag shows **surprise** or **sarcasm**.

- Oh, $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{are leaving}}}$, $\boxed{\color{#f85149}{\text{are}}}$ $\boxed{\color{#2f81f7}{\text{you}}}$?

## 3. Responding to Tag Questions

- ✅ Respond to the **sentence stem**.
- ❌ Don't respond to the **tag**.

### If you agree

| Sentence stem | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| positive | **yes** |
| negative | **no** |

- You like hot weather, don't you? → **Yes**, I do.
- You don't like cold weather, do you? → **No**, I don't.

### If you disagree

| Sentence stem | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| positive | **no** |
| negative | **yes** |

- You like hot weather, don't you? → **No**, I don't.
- You don't like cold weather, do you? → **Yes**, I do.
